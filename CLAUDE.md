## UI guidelines (dashboard/public)

- Plain ES modules and CSS, no build step and no dependencies. Do not add a framework, component library or table engine for looks.
- One token layer: the `:root` block at the top of `styles.css` (surfaces, text, lines, the one accent, status colors, type, spacing, row heights, motion). The look is a warm, light research desk with Linear-style ergonomics: the paper background, white cards and rows, crisp zinc text, hairline rules, pastel status tints (never bright fills), one ink-blue accent, no shadows except on menus and dialogs. There is no separate dark redesign: the light desk is the design. Light is the default (nothing stored means light); dark is `data-theme="dark"` or `"system"`, chosen per browser in Settings and applied before paint by `theme.js`. Components use tokens, never raw colors.
- Type: the system sans for reading and numbers (tabular, lining figures), the book serif (`--font-serif`) for page titles, the inspector's project name, dialogs and long reports, monospace only for identifiers and compared numbers (every figure in the list, the status strip and the inspector's overview, right-aligned). Labels are sentence case, never all caps. No downloaded fonts.
- Icons are local: inline SVG from `ui.js` `icon()` in components, CSS masks (`--i-*`) for callouts, alerts and controls. Project logos are CoinGecko's own images cached by the server (`market-data/logos/<id>.lg.<ext>`) and served from `/api/logos`; without one, a monogram; in the list every logo sits in the same 24px stone circle with a hairline, and nothing is drawn on it.
- Meaning is never color alone: every state is a word (Queued, Working, Waiting, Paused, Check, Failed, Withheld), a sign and a surface (withheld hatched, none dashed, waiting amber with a slow pulse dot, working blue with a pulse dot, failed red), and the accessible label says it in full. Keep the vocabulary in `ui.js` (`RESEARCH_STATE`, `badgeInfo`). Graphics only repeat words that are already there: the grade ruler, the coverage meter, the evidence bar, the progress track, the BTC breadth bar.
- The watchlist shows only values the desk supplies: unknown stays "—" with the reason in its label, stale prices show their age, unknown values sort last both ways. Each model keeps its own grade in its own column; never a combined score. Evidence flags sit beside the grade they concern as a small muted dot (filled = serious, a ring = material; a serious flag also drops the band tint); the words are in the label, the tooltip and the inspector. Grade bands are muted (sage, teal, stone, soft amber, rust); rose is kept for Check (a possibly billed call), never for a grade.
- Holdings live in Portfolio. No research view of positions.
- The project inspector is `?p=<id>` on the watchlist: a dialog (docked beside the list from 1280px, a drawer below that, full screen at phone width). Back, Escape and its close button close it and return focus to the project. Its detail parts come from `views/inspector.js`, built from the project page's components.
- Live updates redraw in place: a view returns its mounted page to update it, keeps focus, open details, scroll and drafts, and never reorders rows while you read (offer Re-sort). One bounded poll in `app.js`; no timer or connection per component.
- The status strip is one line under the title: prices with the rate-limit badge and its countdown beside Refresh, BTC and market figures, models, what needs action (named in words, never only a count) and the review queue. Narrower widths drop secondary figures or wrap; never a stack of banners.
- Keyboard: / or Ctrl/Cmd+K search, J/K (or Up/Down within a column) between rows, Space or Enter on a project toggles its inspector (from 1280px a peek: focus stays on the row and J/K make the inspector follow), Escape closes. Never while typing; no grid roles.
- Sizes: desktop rows 48px (52px on touch), 13px names and figures in the list, tabular numerals, model badges one fixed width (--badge-w, ranges may grow); touch targets 44px; transitions about 140ms and none with reduced motion. Check 1440px and 390px with fictional fixtures (no paid research).
