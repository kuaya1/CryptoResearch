## UI guidelines (dashboard/public)

- Plain ES modules and CSS, no build step and no dependencies. Do not add a framework, component library or table engine for looks.
- One token layer: the `:root` block at the top of `styles.css` (surfaces, text, lines, the one accent, status colors, type, spacing, row heights, motion). The look is a warm, light research desk with Linear-style ergonomics: a stone-50 desk, white cards and rows, warm stone text and hairlines, muted pastel status tints (never bright fills), one ink-blue accent, no shadows except on menus and dialogs. 13px base text, 14px card titles, 32px form fields. There is no separate dark redesign: the light desk is the design. Light is the default (nothing stored means light); dark is `data-theme="dark"` or `"system"`, chosen per browser in Settings and applied before paint by `theme.js`. Components use tokens, never raw colors.
- Type: the system sans (`--sans`) for reading and for every number (tabular, lining figures), page titles 20px/600; the book serif (`--font-serif`) only for the wordmark; monospace (`--mono`) only for contract addresses, hashes and exchange symbols (such as BINANCE:SYRUPUSDT). Labels are sentence case, never all caps. No downloaded fonts (no Inter).
- Icons are local: inline SVG from `ui.js` `icon()` in components, CSS masks (`--i-*`) for callouts, alerts and controls. Project logos are CoinGecko's own images cached by the server (`market-data/logos/<id>.lg.<ext>`) and served from `/api/logos`; without one, a monogram; in the list every logo sits in the same 24px stone circle with a hairline, and nothing is drawn on it.
- No explanatory copy on screen: no page subtitles, legends, disclaimers or "how this works" paragraphs. A card's note is its title's tooltip; a field's hint is its tooltip and screen-reader description (`ui.js` `card`, `field`). Units belong in labels ("(days)"); money a step can spend stays visible. Nothing floats over the list: no hover cards.
- Meaning is never color alone: every state is a word (Queued, Working, Waiting, Paused, Check, Failed, Withheld), a sign and a surface (withheld hatched, none dashed, waiting amber with a slow pulse dot, working blue with a pulse dot, failed red), and the accessible label says it in full. Keep the vocabulary in `ui.js` (`RESEARCH_STATE`, `badgeInfo`). Graphics only repeat words that are already there: the grade ruler, the coverage meter, the evidence bar, the progress track, the BTC breadth bar.
- The watchlist table follows CoinMarketCap: # (rank), Name (24px logo, bold name, ticker), Price, 24h %, 7d %, 30d %, Market Cap, Volume(24h), 7D Chart, then Claude, GPT, Gemini. The desk's own tokens only (rises `--pos-text`, falls `--neg`, hover `--row-hover`; no CoinMarketCap palette); sans figures with even-width digits; one formatter with the inspector (`ui.js` `fmtChangePct`, `fmtMarketUsd`; `watchlist.js` `marketStrings`): changes as a signed percent with two decimals, compact USD with two decimals; 56px rows; a header that stays under the bar. The 7-day chart is one SVG path from `ui.js` `priceSpark` (no library). Page overflow is `clip`, never `hidden`, so sticky elements work.
- The watchlist shows only values the desk supplies: unknown stays "—" with the reason in its label, stale prices show their age, unknown values sort last both ways. Each model keeps its own grade in its own column; never a combined score. Every score uses one 0–100 spectrum on the rubric anchors (`ui.js` `scoreTier`, `--t1..t5-*` tokens): below 40 rose, to 59 amber, to 74 stone, to 89 sage, from 90 jade; a range takes its low end's tier. Model pills (list and inspector tabs) show the figure or the word only, no letters, in the grade's tier, all in 12px monospace (a range keeps its fit; long state words a little tighter); states are stone, Waiting amber, Check and Failed rose; no report is a muted "—" (the label says "no report yet") and On hold plain muted text. At most one mark, as a corner dot inside the pill (newer-run problem, evidence flag: filled serious or a ring material, newer run working, stale). Category bars fill in their own tier. A range is shown as its two numbers, never a midpoint. The words for all of it are in the label, the tooltip and the inspector.
- Holdings live in Portfolio. No research view of positions.
- The inspector header: logo, the full name (it wraps, up to three lines, never cut short) with its symbol as a small pill, then menu and close; link pills (CG, DexScreener, and Web with its icon) on the price line under it; a link the desk does not hold is left out, never a disabled pill or a guessed URL; DexScreener is the verified contract's page from the index row's screen summary, else a ticker search; no CoinMarketCap links or prompts anywhere (a pasted CoinMarketCap link still works as a search lead); then the price with a 24h pill and six tiles (Rank, MC, Vol 24h, FDV, and DefiLlama's 30D Fees and 30D Revenue as saved; a partial month is not a total), or four in one row when no DefiLlama entity is bound, then the contract line (`watchlist.js` `contractStrip`, from the index row's `screening`, never fetched on a read; one 30px line from 641px so J/K never moves the drawer, changes and severe flags first, the rest in its tooltip): the chain and short address of the contracts on record (+N more), then GoPlus's answers across them as a key and a word: Mint, Blacklist (Freeze on Solana) and Proxy as Yes / No / "—" (unknown, dashed; present amber), the highest sell tax (rose above 10%), the worst severe flag (rose), "Changed" with the date for a recent `contract_change`, and "GoPlus <age>" (a clock when stale). A state without a screen is one word: Native coin, None on record, Identity pending, Not screened yet, Not screenable, Screen failed, Not in GoPlus. Under "Contract checks" (`inspector.js` `contractChecks`) every check per contract in words, present first, the unanswered ones on one "No answer" line, taxes, owner, source and GoPlus's own note as data. Nothing is ever labeled safe; the research packet block stays in `packet_mode: "monitor"` until Eric switches it. The investment grade is a half-circle gauge (`ui.js` `gradeGauge`) with the number and the rubric word inside.
- The project inspector is `?p=<id>` on the watchlist: a dialog (docked beside the list from 1280px, a drawer below that, full screen at phone width). Back, Escape and its close button close it and return focus to the project. Its detail parts come from `views/inspector.js`, built from the project page's components.
- DexScreener (`views/dex.js`) is asked by the browser on demand only, never on load, in the background or by the server, and nothing from it is stored. Asked only for a verified identity's contracts on chains it lists, chain-scoped (`token-pairs/v1/<chain>/<address>`, never the cross-chain endpoint, which mixes in other tokens at the same address), 250 ms after the inspector rests. The DEX pill links the contract's page (else a search); the DEX liquidity card at the top of the inspector body shows the deepest pool, its liquidity ("thin" below $100K), 24h volume, buys and sells, and FDV over liquidity. Pools are ranked among the token's own markets, leaving out any priced over 2× away from its CoinGecko price (else the pools' median). The technical chart (`views/chart.js`) shows that pool when no TradingView symbol is set and DEXs carry at least half the 24h volume (or CoinGecko has none); the chart header reads "Technical chart · TradingView" (or DexScreener) with the symbol as a badge that opens the symbol field; a ticker guess carries the amber tag "Auto-pair · unverified".
- Live updates redraw in place: a view returns its mounted page to update it, keeps focus, open details, scroll and drafts, and never reorders rows while you read (offer Re-sort). One bounded poll in `app.js`; no timer or connection per component.
- The status strip is one line under the title in three groups with one separator (1px × 16px): [prices with the rate-limit badge and its countdown, Refresh] | [BTC with its 7d change, dominance, total market cap with its 24h change; each change names its window, as the snapshot has no 24h BTC change] | [breadth: n of N beat BTC over 30d (the 100 largest by CoinGecko market cap, less pegged, gold-backed, fund and wrapped tokens), what needs action (named in words, never only a count), the review queue]. The models' routes are in Settings and each model's tab. The inspector's price time is the bar's refresh time when that refresh stored the price, else "Not refreshed · <age>" (`priceClock`). When it does not fit (the inspector open, a narrower window), `watchlist.js` `fitStrip` drops secondary figures in a fixed order (`STRIP_FIT`, market breadth last) and only then wraps; nothing is cut off mid-word; never a stack of banners.
- Keyboard: / or Ctrl/Cmd+K search, J/K (or Up/Down within a column) between rows, Space or Enter on a project toggles its inspector (from 1280px a peek: focus stays on the row and J/K make the inspector follow), Escape closes. Never while typing; no grid roles.
- Sizes: watchlist rows 56px, 14px names and figures in the list, tabular numerals, model pills one fixed width (`--badge-w`, 56px, ranges included); touch targets 44px; transitions about 140ms and none with reduced motion. Check 1440px and 390px with fictional fixtures (no paid research).
