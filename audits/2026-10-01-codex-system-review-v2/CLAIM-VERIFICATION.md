### What a render and interaction prove

The sweep constructs nineteen common routes plus eight per project. Six demo projects yield 67 routes × five widths = 335; twenty-six fictional projects yield 227 × two = 454; two real projects yield 35 × two = 70. Each route uses a full `/?sw=N#...` navigation, waits briefly for a heading/breadcrumb, and checks some main text, console errors, page-width overflow and selected failure text. It does not assert the final hash equals the requested route or fully validate each record, form submission or report. A navigation deadline/old ready content can also weaken a “ready” test. The recorded results remain useful layout evidence, with that scope.

Interaction scripts perform real UI actions, then record boolean assertions such as focus, geometry, selected URL and receipt text. They are stronger than renders for those flows, but counts include several assertions per action. They use fictional/local services. Console silence says little about pending fetches, charged requests, unreachable queued work or retained sockets. Independent lifecycle instrumentation and durable-record checks are therefore essential.
